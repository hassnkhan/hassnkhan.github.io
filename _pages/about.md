I am currently a New Product Introduction Operations Program Manager at Apple. Previously, I worked for McKinsey's San Francisco Office where I served clients in the consumer electronics and semiconductor manufacturing industries.

I received my PhD from Carnegie Mellon's department of Engineering and Public Policy in 2017. My doctoral work focused on the semiconductor industry's response to the end of CMOS scaling. I examined the Nanoelectronics Research Initiative's attempt to identify alternatives to silicon CMOS. More broadly my work examined the historical evolution of the industry's collaborative institutions and their struggle to respond to the looming technological discontinuity. My work was published in [Nature Electronics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41928-017-0005-9), please reach out to me directly for a copy.
