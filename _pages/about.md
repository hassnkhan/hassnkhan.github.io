I am currently a New Product Introduction Operations Program Manager at Apple.
Translation: I work with engineering to make sure our new products are ready to
ramp and with our worldwide readiness teams to make sure we are ready for launch.
Previously, I worked for McKinsey's San Francisco Office where I served
clients in the consumer electronics and semiconductor manufacturing industries.

My doctoral work focused on the semiconductor industry's response to the end of
CMOS scaling. I examined the Nanoelectronics Research Initiative's attempt to
identify alternatives to silicon CMOS. More broadly my work examined the
historical evolution of the industry's collaborative institutions and their
struggle to respond to the looming technological discontinuity.
