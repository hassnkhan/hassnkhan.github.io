My work spans semiconductors, technology, and industrial policy. I am interested in how industries develop new technologies, organize production, and respond to technological change.

## Experience

I served as a Senior Policy Advisor on the Strategy Team at the CHIPS Program Office, U.S. Department of Commerce.

Previously, I was a New Product Introduction Operations Program Manager at Apple, where I led operations for the launch of the HomePod mini colors in 2021 and the HomePod in early 2023.

At McKinsey’s San Francisco office from 2018 to 2020, I worked with consumer electronics and semiconductor manufacturers on supply chains, procurement, and organizational change.

I began my career as a process and integration engineer at Twin Creeks Technologies, developing solar-cell manufacturing processes and transferring a metallization process from R&D to pilot-scale production.

## Research & education

I earned my PhD in Engineering and Public Policy at Carnegie Mellon in 2017. My research examined the semiconductor industry’s response to the end of CMOS scaling, including the Nanoelectronics Research Initiative and the evolution of the industry’s collaborative institutions.

My work was published in [Nature Electronics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41928-017-0005-9). [Email me](mailto:hassnkhan@gmail.com) for a copy.

I received my BS in Chemical Engineering from UC Berkeley in 2010. Go Bears!
